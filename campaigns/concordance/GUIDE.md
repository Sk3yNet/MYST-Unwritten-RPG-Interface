# The Concordance: GM's campaign guide

*Spoilers: this is the GM's bible. Everything here is also on the GM Screen, scene by scene.*

The explorers wake in the Cartographer's Sanctuary, a library built around a machine called the Concordance. Five
Linking Books lead to five elemental Ages. Each Age expresses **one rule of reality**, and each yields five clues.
Only together do the clues explain how to wake the Concordance.

| Age | Rule | Key unlocks | Glyph | Tone | Constellation → ring | Journal's last line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frozen Observatory (ice) | **Preserve** | Fire case | snowflake (symmetric) | E | Lantern → ring 1 (inner) | “It is already right. Do not turn it.” |
| Ember Forge (fire) | **Transform** | Air case | flame (leans right) | C | Anvil → ring 2 | “…each one wakes as the opposite of what you set.” |
| Sky Monastery (air) | **Observe** | Earth case + the balcony lift | spiral | G | Kite → ring 3 | “Climb. Look down. Then look again.” |
| Verdant Labyrinth (earth) | **Grow** | Water case | mirrored spiral | D | Seed → ring 4 | “…outward, ring by ring, as roots grow.” |
| Tidal Cathedral (water) | **Reflect** | stills the pool | drop (mirror of the flame) | A | Heron → ring 5 (outer) | “…the fire turns every glyph to its twin.” |

**Twins:** Fire and Water are mirror images; so are Air and Earth. Ice is its own reflection. This is the heart of
the design: *reflecting* a glyph and *transforming* it give the same result.

## The final puzzle

The Concordance has five concentric rings. Each ring shows one of five glyphs and has a lever. Each rim is
punched with star dots; the constellation charts identify them.

1. **Ice, Preserve.** The inner ring (Lantern) is frozen and already shows Ice. Don't turn it; just pull its lever:
   it sings **E**.
2. **Fire, Transform.** Pulling a ring's lever flips its glyph to its twin. Set the twin of what you want.
3. **Earth, Grow.** Pulling a lever also nudges the next ring outward one glyph. So set a ring only *after* waking the
   one inside it: center outward, one at a time.
4. **Air, Observe.** The answer is painted on the dome, readable only from the Star Gallery balcony (lift powered by
   the Air Key).
5. **Water, Reflect.** The dome is painted mirror-wise. In the still pool (stilled by the Water Key) it shows what each
   ring must **become**; read directly, it shows what to **set**.

| Ring | Constellation | Dome, direct (set this) | Dome, reflected (becomes this) | Chime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (inner) | Lantern | Ice (don't turn) | Ice | E |
| 2 | Anvil | Fire | Water | C |
| 3 | Kite | Air | Earth | G |
| 4 | Seed | Water | Fire | D |
| 5 (outer) | Heron | Earth | Air | A |

The finished melody is **E – C – G – D – A**. Wrong rings ring sour; a reset wheel clears rings 2–5 at a minor cost.
Both readings of the dome lead to the right answer if the players understand the rules, so a new GM never has to
rule on an ambiguous interpretation. If the table is stuck, run it as a **deduction** (“How does the Concordance want
to be set?”).

## The Ages, puzzle by puzzle

### 1. The Frozen Observatory (Ice: Preserve)
* **Mirror Terrace:** Mirror A takes the sun → B or C. B → the Hall's ice wall, or the Dome. C → the cistern dam.
  A→B→Hall melts the wall into the Star Archive; leave it lit too long and the Hall floods (delay or *Soaked to the
  Bone*). Turning a mirror: Athletics/Engineering vs Fair.
* **Star Archive:** the Lantern chart, a brass Concordance model with a frozen inner ring, a tuning fork that sings E,
  journal page.
* **Master: the Great Dome.** Open the Lantern shutter (third from the door; the Hall's telescopes all point there)
  and send the beam via B. Wrong shutter: *Cracked Lens* (Craft vs Great to repair). Reward: Ice Key.

### 2. The Ember Forge (Fire: Transform)
* **Valves:** A heats the boiler (+1 pressure mark a minute), B heats the foundry crucible. The lift needs pressure
  2–4 plus the counterweight released; above 4 the vent blows (*Scalded*). Open A → wait for 3 → release → ride up →
  close A, open B. Opening B cools the lower lava channel into a bridge to the Anvil Shrine.
* **Master: the Bell Foundry.** Molds are negatives: the **Water** mold casts a **flame** bell. Ring it (C): the slag
  on the anvil cracks. Reward: Fire Key. Anvil constellation in the shrine.
* **Tamsin Hale** (optional rival) arrives here.

### 3. The Sky Monastery (Air: Observe)
* **Prayer wheels:** I turns North, II turns South, III turns East. The monk's map (upside down; players can press
  *Turn over*) gives stars per tower: **I ×2, II ×3, III ×1.**
* **Belfry:** from the listening seat, the aligned towers' panels form the Air glyph; the chord sounds and the deepest
  chime sings G. Journal under the seat.
* **Master: the Summit Shrine** opens to the chord. The Kite is laid in the floor, meant to be read from above (from
  the North Tower), which is the hint about the Sanctuary's balcony. Reward: Air Key (powers the lift).

### 4. The Verdant Labyrinth (Earth: Grow)
* **Gardens:** Stairbloom (water) → stairs; Ladder-reed (sun) → ladder into the Heart Tree; Bridgevine (water + sun)
  → bridge over the Ravine, but it chokes the middle sluice, so grow it last; Thornwall walls off the Ravine (don't).
  Order: “Rain, then Sun, then both.”
* **Master: the Calendar Stone.** Five rings of seasons (Rain, Sun, Rain & Sun, Growth, Rest). Turning a ring
  advances it and the ring outside it. Start (inner→outer): Sun, Rest, Rain, Growth, Rain & Sun. Solve from the heart:
  **ring 1 ×3, ring 2 ×2, ring 3 ×2, ring 4 ×4, ring 5 ×3.** The stone drums D. Reward: Earth Key; the Seed is carved
  in the Heart Tree.

### 5. The Tidal Cathedral (Water: Reflect)
* **Tide Wheel:** Low (the Moon Pool niche is dry: journal page), Mid (still water, reflections, the waterline bell
  rings A), High (the crypt's painted arch becomes an underwater passage to the Apse).
* **Moon Pool:** mirror-written rim: SEE IT ONLY IN ITS REFLECTION.
* **Master: the Apse.** Three ceiling glyphs painted mirror-wise. Directly: Water, Air, Ice. In the reflection (Mid
  tide): **Fire, Earth, Ice**, which opens the reliquary. Reward: Water Key (stills the Sanctuary pool). Heron on
  the ceiling.

## Pacing

Roughly one to two sessions per Age, one for the prologue, one for the finale. Each Age follows the same rhythm:
arrive → find the central mechanic → two or three small puzzles that teach it → journal and clues → master puzzle and
Key → return home, set the Key, open the next Book. Award a **minor milestone** each session and a **significant
milestone** after each Age; the finale earns a **major milestone**.

## Changing things

The maps and handouts are drawn by `source/art.py` from tables at the top of the file (glyphs, constellations, tones,
ring order, dome layout). Change a table, run `python3 source/art.py`, and every map and handout follows. Keep the
glyph twins (fire/water, air/earth) intact, or the final puzzle stops working.
