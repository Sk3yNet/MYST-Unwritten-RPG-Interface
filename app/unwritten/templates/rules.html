Rolling

Roll four Drama dice (each −, blank or +), add them up, and add your skill. Anything not on your sheet is Mediocre (+0). Then subtract the opposition: a fixed difficulty, or someone else’s roll. The difference is your shifts.

Aspects & drama points

  • Invoke: after rolling, spend a drama point on a relevant aspect for +2 or a reroll. Several aspects can each be invoked once per roll; free invokes (from advantages) can stack on one aspect.
  • Compel: an aspect complicates your life. Accept and gain a drama point, or pay one to avoid it.
  • Declare a detail: spend a drama point to add a convenient story detail justified by your aspects (GM may veto).
  • Boosts: one-use, short-lived invokes. They can’t be compelled or paid for, but can be promoted to a full aspect.
  • Refresh: each session drama points refill up to refresh (extras kept); each chapter they reset to it.

  • You take one from an opponent’s excelled advantage, from succeeding at a serious cost, or by sacrificing when you’re out of drama points (the GM gets a free invoke).
  • A consequence with no open slot rolls up to the next open one; with none left you’re overwhelmed and out of the scene.
  • Recovery: succeed at an overcome against the listed difficulty (+1 if treating yourself) to mark it recovering. Recovering minors clear at scene end; minors and moderates at the next session; all at a new chapter.
  • Dangerous actions: fumble one and you’re overwhelmed.

Characters

  • High concept, four journey aspects, and three consequence slots (minor, moderate, severe).
  • Skills: one Great (+4), two Good (+3), three Fair (+2), four Adequate (+1). Afterwards no rank may hold more skills than the rank below it.
  • Three free stunts or resources; up to two more at one refresh each. Refresh starts at 3 and never drops below 1.
  • Skills: {{ rules.SKILLS | join(", ") }}.

Teamwork

The highest-rated helper rolls, +1 for each helper with at least Adequate in a similar skill; everyone shares any cost. Or stack advantages and pass the free invokes to one person.

Deductions

  1. Frame the question; each participant gives a reason they can help.
  2. Three rounds: everyone rolls a skill vs Good (+3), later vs the previous round’s winning total. The best success wins: +2 progress on an excel, +1 on a success, +1 and a minor cost on a tie. The winner declares a fact (a step toward the answer, not the answer).
  3. With 3+ progress everyone rolls once more; the highest declares the truth as an aspect: 3 progress no free invokes, 4–5 one, 6 two. The facts fold into it. With less, the question stays open.

Discover

Ask one question per shift. When you run out you must change the situation (an advantage, a helper, time away, a new angle) to dig again.
